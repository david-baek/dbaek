David Baek

Hi, I’m David. I’m a PhD student at MIT EECS .

My research focuses on scalable oversight and alignment of LLMs. As AI systems become increasingly powerful at an unprecedented speed, it may be tempting to rely on defensive methods, such as catching malicious behaviors via CoT monitoring, or building unbreakable sandboxes. I believe these methods will expose us to a huge unpredictable threat surface. My research goal is to solve inner alignment : to study how to build or construct provably safe AI systems. Most recently, I’m exploring how to edit beliefs of LLMs in a provable verified manner.
